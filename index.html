FAQ

Questions firms ask us.

How does the 30-in-30 guarantee work?

Once your campaign goes live, we deliver 30 qualified leads, exclusive to your firm, within 30 days. If we don't hit 30, you don't pay for the pilot. Any lead with a bad number, outside your states or a duplicate is replaced free. We guarantee delivery of leads; how many sign is up to your intake team.

Which states do you work in?

We run campaigns in the states you're licensed to take cases in. Lead generation rules vary by state, so we confirm your markets on the strategy call before anything launches.

How are leads delivered?

In real time, the moment a lead qualifies — by email, text, or directly into your CRM. Each lead includes the answers they gave during screening.

How much does it cost?

You pay per lead. Pricing depends on case type and the states you want to cover, and we go through it on the call.

Do we get to see how the leads are generated?

Yes. We show you the ads, the landing pages and the screening questions. There's nothing about your lead flow you won't be able to see.

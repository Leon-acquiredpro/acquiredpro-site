Privacy Policy

Last updated September 13, 2026

This policy explains how Lwin Solutions Pro LLC, doing business as Acquired Pro ("Acquired Pro", "we", "us"), collects and uses information when you visit acquiredpro.io or contact us about our services. Acquired Pro is a marketing company that provides lead generation services to law firms. We are not a law firm and do not provide legal advice.

Information we collect

  • Information you submit through our forms, such as your name, email, phone number, firm name, practice areas and the states you operate in.
  • Scheduling details when you book a call.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type, pages visited and referring source, collected through cookies and similar technologies, including advertising pixels.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule and conduct calls.
  • To send you information about our services, where you have agreed to receive it.
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising.

Text messages

If you opt in to receive text messages from Acquired Pro, we use your mobile number to send messages about your inquiry, scheduled calls and our services. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All categories above exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

How we share it

We share information with service providers that help us operate, such as our CRM, scheduling, email and advertising platforms, only as needed to provide our services. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and advertising

We use cookies and advertising technologies, including the Meta Pixel, to understand site traffic and measure campaigns. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Retention and your choices

We keep information for as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or unsubscribe from communications at any time, by emailing us.

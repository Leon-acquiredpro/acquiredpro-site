The screening

What every claimant answers before you see them.

This is the full question list. When we launch your campaign, we walk you through the live page and adjust the questions to match your intake criteria.

  1. What happenedSomething that happened while they were working, as opposed to pay or firing issues
  2. When it happenedWithin the last month, 1–12 months, or 1–2 years ago
  3. How they were paidW-2 employee, 1099 contractor, or not sure
  4. Federal or Postal Service jobFederal workers use a separate program, not state workers' comp
  5. Missed workWhether it has kept them off the job
  6. Existing representationWhether they already have a lawyer for it
  7. StateMatched to the states you take cases in
  8. In their own wordsA short description, so your intake team knows the story before they call
  9. Contact + consentName, mobile, email, and consent to be contacted by your firm, with a timestamped certificate

Never billed to you

These claimants are screened out before a lead is counted:

  • Already represented by a lawyer
  • Happened more than two years ago
  • Federal government or U.S. Postal Service job
  • Paid as a 1099 contractor
  • Outside your states
  • Wrong number or duplicate: replaced free

FAQ

Workers' comp questions firms ask us.

Are the leads exclusive?

Yes. Each claimant is sent to one firm only. We don't resell leads or send them to a panel of firms.

Which states do you cover?

The states you're licensed to take cases in. Rules for lead generation differ from state to state, so we confirm your markets on the strategy call before anything launches.

Can we change the screening questions?

Yes. If your firm only wants recent claims, or has its own rules for contractors, we change the questions and the disqualifiers before launch.

How much does it cost?

You pay per lead. Pricing depends on your states and your volume. We cover it on the call, after we know what a qualified lead means for your firm.
